# A new home for my writing

I wanted this site to feel like my old homepage again: simple, personal, and easy to keep current.

The new version gives me one place to collect what I write, whether a piece lives here or somewhere else. There is less machinery, more room for words, and a small stack of cards on the homepage pointing to the latest things.

That feels like a good place to start.
